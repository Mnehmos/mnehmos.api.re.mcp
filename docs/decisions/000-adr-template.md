# ADR template

Copy for each new decision as `NNN-short-slug.md`.

---

## NNN. Title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are in play; the constraint or defect that demands a decision.
Cite measurements where they exist.

## Decision

The decision, stated in one sentence, then in detail.

## Alternatives considered

What was rejected and **why** — rejected approaches are part of the memory
system.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, accepted trade-offs.

## Review trigger

The condition under which this decision must be revisited.
